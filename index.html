01 / 06

Asian Paints

Campaign & Experiential Design

"Yaadon Se Ranga Har Ghar" — a home coloured by memories.

Asian Paints wanted a campaign that connected emotionally rather than just showing off colour cards. I conceived the Maze of Memories — a walk-through mall activation where every turn recreates a different era of the Indian home, down to period-accurate walls, textures, décor and music, before inviting visitors to reimagine their own home with Asian Paints. Deliverables spanned experience architecture, a working scale prototype, competitive positioning against Berger, Nerolac and Dulux, and OOH/billboard concepts designed for placement outside malls like Phoenix Palladium.

RoleCampaign concept, experience design, prototype build, billboard & poster design
Experience Design OOH / Billboard Competitive Strategy
02 / 06

IKEA

Emerging Tech & Product-Service Design

The Future of Flexible Living.

Brief: help IKEA make furniture more adaptable and sustainable as customers' needs change. I designed a system around three moves — an AR/VR preview tool so customers can see a piece in their own space before buying, a "Swap & Save" in-app take-back flow that rewards returned furniture with points toward a curated Material Portfolio of sustainable swaps, and IoT/RFID tracking that simplifies assembly and cuts loss in shipping. The concept closed with a trust-building ad campaign built on real family scenarios, countering perceptions of low durability.

RoleService design, UX flow & UI design, sustainability strategy
Service Design AR/VR Sustainability
03 / 06

Vegas Vendetta

Game Design · Team Project

A gangster-vs-cops bluffing game, built on a workplace-stress framework.

Built with a four-person team, starting from a blunt statistic: 36% of managers report burnout and 62% of employees feel stress from colleagues. We translated that tension into a social-deduction board game set in a mob-run Vegas, where players bet blind on who's holding the correct answer, and the highest bet wins when more than one player is right. I worked on the theme, board and card system design, and the full physical prototype — board, custom card decks, tokens, dice and guidebook — presented live to faculty.

RoleConcept & game mechanics, physical prototyping, live presentation
Game Mechanics Physical Prototyping Team Project
04 / 06

Seva Stall

Service Design

A one-stop service center for India's street food vendors.

India has roughly 10 million street vendors, and the Street Vendors Act of 2014 meant to protect them is weakly enforced — leaving vendors exposed to eviction, bribery and unfair fines. I designed Seva Stall, a walk-in digital licensing and support centre where vendors can get trade licences, health permits, loans and business education in one place, verified and tracked transparently. The service was shaped with input from Prof. Jai Ranjit on government and street-food-sector policy, and delivered as a full physical floor plan, digital licensing counters, a vendor ID system, and supporting verification and payment collateral.

RoleService design, stakeholder research, floor plan & service collateral
Social Impact Spatial Design Policy Research
05 / 06

Agoda

Experiential & Exhibition Design

A 360° immersive travel-expo booth.

Designed a circular exhibition stall for Agoda built around a rotating wheel of destinations, an AR screen and hologram at its centre, and a rotating tile wall for browsing deals. A mascot, Agoji, guides visitors from the expo gates to the stall through a step-by-step in-app map, and the booth doubles as a small retail moment with branded merchandise — an inflatable neck pillow, fidget bag charms — and social-media-ready photo mockups.

RoleSpatial & exhibition design, technical sketching, mascot-led wayfinding
Exhibition Design Wayfinding UX Merchandise
06 / 06

EnAble India

Strategic Planning & Social Impact

Flipping hiring so employers pitch to talent — not the other way round.

EnAble India is a Bangalore non-profit that has helped place persons with disabilities in jobs since 1999. The root problem I found: employers simply don't know what candidates with disabilities can do. My response was a Reverse Hiring Event service — candidates get accessible application support and a vetting panel to prepare their pitch, while employers must pass an inclusion audit before they're allowed to pitch to candidates at all. It reframes hiring from charity to a shared, accountable, dignity-first process, with mentorship built in after the hire.

RoleStrategic design, service blueprinting, stakeholder framework design
Service Blueprint Accessibility Social Impact
